# wpark-nn-integration

 A streamlit python based simulation of car parks to train a genetic neural network to efficiently assign carparks to cars based on purpose of parking (such as
 shopping, emergency medical response etc.), proximity of car park to desired destination i.e. a particular shop, time of stay and predicted time of stay (i.e. predicting  
the time with potential extension based on purpose of stay), car size, accessibility and so on to reduce congestion and assign more efficient parking spaces to reduce      
travelling of patrons.
